Welcome to the Quantitative Computer Architecture Simulation Laboratory.

These simulations provide an interactive environment for investigating how architectural design decisions affect computer performance.

Students will manipulate architectural parameters, conduct experiments, analyze quantitative results, and use those results to support architectural decisions.
